# Sources — the news of Monday 23 November 2026

Every story in the image happened on, or was first reported / confirmed on,
23 November 2026. Stories that belonged to earlier days were deliberately
left out (see the note at the bottom).

1. **LEAD — Dymer, Kyiv region, Ukraine.** The death toll from a Russian
   strike on a five-storey apartment block in Dymer, about 45 km north of
   Kyiv, rose to 11 on Monday as rescuers recovered another body from the
   rubble; a separate strike on a supermarket in Vyshneve killed three more,
   and the Kyiv region declared a day of mourning.
   — Reuters, "Russian attack on Ukraine's Dymer in Kyiv region kills 11,
   official says", 23 Nov 2026; AFP wire reports the same day (rescue in
   Dymer, toll rising through Monday morning).

2. **Indonesia factory fire.** Indonesia confirmed on Monday that 24 people
   died in the fire at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia confectionery factory in
   Cikande, Banten province, with about 30 people still missing as rescuers
   searched the unstable wreckage. (The blaze broke out Saturday evening;
   the confirmed toll of 24 is Monday's news.)
   — Reuters, "Indonesia says 24 killed in factory blaze as rescuers search
   for dozens missing", Jakarta, 23 Nov 2026.

3. **Markets — AI sell-off.** Fears that AI shares are overpriced rattled
   world markets: Japan's Nikkei 225 fell 5.06%, the S&P 500 fell 3.7% and
   gold rose 2.72% as investors sought safer assets.
   — One Turn world-news edition, Monday 23 Nov 2026 (market figures);
   SKN Finance and Seeking Alpha market reports of the same day (Asian
   markets slide in Monday trade as the AI sell-off spreads).

4. **Belgium — airspace shutdown.** Belgian air traffic controllers joined
   a nationwide anti-austerity day of action on Monday, suspending services
   from 07:00 to 21:00 and cancelling nearly all flights; Brussels Airport
   alone had about 429 flights and 59,500 passengers scheduled, and
   Charleroi cancelled all flights for the day.
   — Travel + Leisure Asia and FTN News reports, 23 Nov 2026 (skeyes
   announcement; Brussels Airport figures); Europe Says wire summary of
   Monday's cancellations.

5. **Hungary — PM to visit Ukraine.** Hungarian Prime Minister Peter Magyar
   said on Monday he would soon travel to the Zakarpattia region of western
   Ukraine to express support for the ethnic Hungarian minority living
   there, and that EU budget negotiations and European financial support
   for Ukraine should be treated separately.
   — Reuters, "Hungary PM Magyar says he will travel to Ukraine 'soon'",
   23 Nov 2026.

6. **Footer brief — Russia's sporting return.** Russia's return to
   international sport after its exclusion over the invasion of Ukraine is
   facing mounting opposition from European nations.
   — Reuters, "Russia's sports comeback hits a European wall", 23 Nov 2026.
